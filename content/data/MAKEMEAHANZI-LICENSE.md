# Make Me a Hanzi data

`hanzi.json` is derived from `dictionary.txt` of Make Me a Hanzi
(https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi), which is itself derived from Unihan
and CJKlib. You can redistribute and/or modify it under the terms of the GNU
Lesser General Public License, version 3 or later
(https://www.gnu.org/licenses/lgpl-3.0.html).

`lookalike-candidates.tsv` was computed by `scripts/build-hanzi.py` from the
stroke outlines in `graphics.txt` (Arphic Public License) and the
decompositions in `dictionary.txt`; no outline data is included.
